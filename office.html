Office of the Deputy Speaker

Serving Through Leadership

The Office of the Deputy Speaker supports the work of the Kogi State House of Assembly while promoting responsible legislative leadership, effective representation and engagement with citizens.

The Office

Office of the Deputy Speaker

The Office of the Deputy Speaker of the Kogi State House of Assembly plays an important role in supporting the leadership and legislative responsibilities of the House. The office works alongside the Speaker and other members to promote effective legislative proceedings and public service.

Key Responsibilities

Leadership & Legislative Service

The office contributes to the effective functioning of the House and promotes constructive engagement with citizens and stakeholders.

⚖️

Legislative Leadership

Supporting the conduct of legislative proceedings and contributing to the effective performance of the Kogi State House of Assembly.

🏛️

House Administration

Working with the leadership and members of the House to support orderly, effective and responsible legislative activities.

🤝

Public Engagement

Encouraging communication and engagement between representatives, constituents, communities and stakeholders.

📋

Representation

Supporting responsible representation of citizens and consideration of issues affecting communities across Kogi State.

🌱

Community Development

Supporting initiatives and partnerships that contribute to community development and improved public welfare.

🔎

Accountability

Promoting responsible public service, transparency and communication with citizens and stakeholders.

What The Office Does

A Platform for Effective Public Service

The Office of the Deputy Speaker provides a channel through which legislative responsibilities, constituency representation and public engagement can be coordinated.

Through legislative participation and engagement with citizens, the office remains committed to responsible leadership and service.

Legislative Participation Participation in debates, motions and legislative proceedings of the House.
Constituency Representation Maintaining communication with constituents and representing their interests.
Stakeholder Engagement Engaging communities, institutions and stakeholders on matters of public interest.
Public Information Providing verified information about official activities and programmes.
Community Support Supporting initiatives that contribute to the wellbeing and development of communities.
Good Governance Promoting responsible leadership, accountability and effective representation.

Connect With The Office

For official enquiries, constituency engagement and public information, citizens and stakeholders can contact the Office of the Deputy Speaker.

Legislative Service

Working Through Committees

Committees are an important part of the legislative process. They provide opportunities for detailed consideration of issues, engagement with relevant stakeholders and oversight of matters within their areas of responsibility.

Legislative Responsibility

Service Through Effective Legislative Engagement

The legislative process requires collaboration, careful consideration of issues and meaningful engagement with citizens and stakeholders.

Through committee activities and other legislative responsibilities, the House can examine matters affecting the people and contribute to effective governance.

  • Legislative consideration and review
  • Oversight and accountability
  • Stakeholder engagement
  • Public hearings and consultations

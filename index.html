About The Deputy Speaker

A Commitment To Public Service

Rt. Hon. Comfort Nwuchiola Ojomah Egwaba serves as Deputy Speaker of the Kogi State House of Assembly and represents Ibaji Local Government Constituency.

Her public service role reflects a commitment to legislative responsibility, constituency representation, constructive engagement and the advancement of democratic governance.

Through this official platform, citizens and stakeholders can access information about the office, legislative activities, constituency engagements, news and public updates.

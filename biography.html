Public Service

A Commitment to Leadership and Service

Rt. Hon. Comfort Nwuchiola Ojomah Egwaba is a Nigerian legislator and public servant serving as the Deputy Speaker of the Kogi State House of Assembly and Member Representing Ibaji Local Government Constituency.

In her capacity as Deputy Speaker, Rt. Hon. Comfort Nwuchiola Ojomah Egwaba contributes to the leadership and effective functioning of the Kogi State House of Assembly.

Her responsibilities include supporting legislative proceedings, promoting constructive deliberations and contributing to the discharge of the Assembly's constitutional responsibilities.

Current Public Office

Position: Deputy Speaker

Institution: Kogi State House of Assembly

Constituency: Ibaji Local Government Constituency

As the representative of Ibaji Local Government Constituency, she is committed to maintaining a strong connection with the people she represents and providing a channel through which the concerns and aspirations of constituents can receive appropriate attention within the legislative process.

Her approach to public service is centred on representation, accountability, responsible leadership and community engagement.

Through her legislative responsibilities, she seeks to contribute to policies and initiatives that support good governance, social development and improved opportunities for the people of Kogi State.

As Deputy Speaker, she also recognises the importance of cooperation among lawmakers, government institutions, traditional and community leaders, civil society and other stakeholders in advancing the interests of the state.

The Office of the Deputy Speaker remains committed to keeping citizens informed about legislative activities, constituency engagement, public programmes, official announcements and other matters of public interest.

Rt. Hon. Comfort Nwuchiola Ojomah Egwaba's public service reflects a commitment to the principles of service, representation, accountability and development, with particular attention to the people of Ibaji Local Government Constituency and the wider interests of Kogi State.

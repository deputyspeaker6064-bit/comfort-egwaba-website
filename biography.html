Official Biography

Rt. Hon. Comfort Ojoma Nwuchiola Egwaba

Deputy Speaker of the Kogi State House of Assembly and Member Representing Ibaji State Constituency.

Rt. Hon. Comfort Ojoma Nwuchiola Egwaba

Rt. Hon. Comfort Ojoma Nwuchiola Egwaba is an accomplished legislator, distinguished public servant, and trailblazing political leader whose exemplary service has continued to shape governance and inspire inclusive leadership in Kogi State and beyond.

She currently serves as the Deputy Speaker of the Kogi State House of Assembly and represents the people of Ibaji State Constituency, making history as the first woman to occupy the office of Deputy Speaker in the State's legislative history.

A native of Ibaji Local Government Area of Kogi State, Rt. Hon. Egwaba has built a record of purposeful leadership, effective representation, grassroots engagement and public service.

Education & Academic Background

A native of Ibaji Local Government Area of Kogi State, Rt. Hon. Egwaba attended Government Girls Secondary School, Idah, before proceeding to the Kogi State College of Health Sciences and Technology, Idah, where she obtained a National Diploma (ND) in Medical Laboratory Technology.

She further obtained a Higher National Diploma (HND) in Medical Laboratory Technology from the Federal School of Medical Laboratory Technology (Science), Jos/Vom, Plateau State, and a Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) in Peace Studies and Conflict Resolution from the National Open University of Nigeria (NOUN).

She is currently pursuing a Master of Science (M.Sc.) in International Relations and Diplomacy at Salem University, Lokoja, Kogi State.

Her strong academic background, combined with years of grassroots engagement and public service, has equipped her with the vision, knowledge and capacity to provide purposeful leadership and effective representation.

Legislative Leadership

Since her election into the 8th Kogi State House of Assembly, Rt. Hon. Egwaba has distinguished herself as a proactive legislator committed to promoting good governance, social justice, and sustainable development.

As Deputy Speaker, she has played a strategic role in strengthening legislative oversight, fostering harmony within the Assembly, and supporting the passage of people-oriented laws and policies that advance the development of Kogi State.

Her legislative record reflects an unwavering commitment to equity, inclusion, and community development.

Legislative Record & Community Development

She sponsored the landmark Kogi State Oil Producing Areas Development Commission (KOSOPADEC) Bill, designed to accelerate the development of oil-producing communities, as well as the Gender Representation Bill aimed at expanding opportunities for women's participation in governance.

She also championed the motion for the reconstruction of the strategic Idah–Ibaji Road and has consistently advocated policies that improve access to quality education, healthcare, agriculture, infrastructure, and economic opportunities for rural communities.

Grassroots & Humanitarian Leadership

Beyond the legislature, Rt. Hon. Egwaba is widely recognised for her people-centred leadership and unwavering dedication to grassroots development.

Through regular constituency engagements, town hall meetings, educational support initiatives, youth and women empowerment programmes, healthcare interventions, peacebuilding efforts, and strategic advocacy for infrastructure development, she has continued to improve the lives of her constituents.

Her humanitarian initiatives through the Comfort Ladies Foundation have further empowered women, youths, widows, and vulnerable persons, reinforcing her enduring commitment to inclusive development and social welfare.

Gender Equality & Democratic Inclusion

An advocate for gender equality and democratic inclusion, Rt. Hon. Egwaba has consistently championed increased representation of women in leadership and governance.

Her commitment to advancing the rights and participation of women has earned her recognition beyond Nigeria's borders, including her participation in the West Africa Female Parliamentarians Peer Review Conference on Ending Violence Against Women and Girls held in Accra, Ghana.

At the conference, she joined female legislators, policymakers, and development partners from across the region to strengthen collaborative efforts towards gender equality and sustainable development.

Awards & Recognition

Her outstanding contributions to public service have attracted widespread recognition.

She was honoured as the Most Outstanding Female Legislator during the Kogi State 34th Anniversary Lecture and Awards organised by the Nigerian Post in 2025.

She has received the Award of Excellence from the Department of English and Literary Studies, Federal University Lokoja, among other commendations from professional bodies, community organisations, youth groups, and civil society organisations.

She was also nominated for the prestigious Margaret Ekpo Memorial Award in recognition of her outstanding contributions to women's leadership and public service.

Traditional Titles & Honour

In recognition of her exceptional leadership, dedication to community development, and service to humanity, Rt. Hon. Comfort Ojoma Nwuchiola Egwaba has been honoured with distinguished traditional titles.

She is the Gimbiya Mata of Girinya and was conferred with the revered title of Iganya Attah-Igala (Adeebu) by His Royal Majesty, Agabaidu, the Attah Igala and President of the Kogi State Traditional Council, in recognition of her remarkable contributions to the development of the Igala Kingdom and Kogi State.

Faith, Integrity & Servant Leadership

A woman of faith, integrity, compassion, and exceptional leadership, Rt. Hon. Comfort Ojoma Nwuchiola Egwaba exemplifies the ideals of servant leadership.

Her unwavering dedication to legislative excellence, grassroots development, peacebuilding, women's empowerment, and public service continues to inspire a new generation of leaders while leaving an enduring legacy of impactful governance.

Leadership Legacy

A Symbol of Courage, Resilience & Visionary Leadership

Rt. Hon. Comfort Ojoma Nwuchiola Egwaba remains a symbol of courage, resilience, and visionary leadership whose contributions to democratic governance and human development have earned her a place among Nigeria's most respected contemporary female legislators.

Her unwavering dedication to legislative excellence, grassroots development, peacebuilding, women's empowerment, and public service continues to inspire a new generation of leaders.

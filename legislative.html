Legislative Service

Serving Through Effective Legislation

The Kogi State House of Assembly plays an important role in making laws, representing citizens and providing legislative oversight in the state.

As Deputy Speaker, Rt. Hon. Comfort Nwuchiola Ojomah Egwaba contributes to the effective functioning of the House and supports the conduct of legislative proceedings.

This section is designed to provide citizens and stakeholders with access to verified information about legislative activities, official engagements and developments.

Legislative work involves discussion, consideration, debate and decisions on matters affecting the public.

Public issues and legislative proposals are brought forward for consideration.

02

The House reaches decisions in accordance with applicable legislative procedures.

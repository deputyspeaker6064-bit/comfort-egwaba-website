About the Deputy Speaker

Rt. Hon. Comfort Nwuchiola Ojomah Egwaba serves as Deputy Speaker of the Kogi State House of Assembly.

In this capacity, the Deputy Speaker contributes to the legislative responsibilities of the House and supports the effective conduct of legislative proceedings.

Her office is focused on responsible representation, constructive legislative engagement, accountability and initiatives that contribute to the development and wellbeing of the people of Kogi State.

This website provides citizens and stakeholders with information about the office, legislative activities, constituency engagement, public programmes and official updates.

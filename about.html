A Commitment To Public Service

The Office of Rt. Hon. Comfort Ojoma Nwuchiola Egwaba is dedicated to effective representation, responsible leadership and meaningful engagement with the people of Ibaji Local Government Constituency.

As Deputy Speaker of the Kogi State House of Assembly and Member representing Ibaji State Constituency, the office serves as a bridge between the people and the legislative institution.

The office is committed to listening to the needs of constituents, supporting initiatives that improve lives and promoting development through effective representation and legislative engagement.

Through regular constituency interaction, legislative activities and community engagement, the office continues to work toward a more inclusive and prosperous future for the people.
